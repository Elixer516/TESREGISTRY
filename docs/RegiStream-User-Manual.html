User Manual

How to operate the Registrar Management Information System as it stands today — written for the three people who use it: the Registrar, the Trainer, and the Trainee. Every control named here is named exactly as it appears on screen.

Version: V9.4 Issued: 9 September 2026 Applies to: elixer516.github.io/TESREGISTRY

Before you begin

Signing in

  1. Open the system in a web browser.
  2. On the sign-in screen, either type your centre email address and password, or click any account in the Demo accounts panel to fill the form for you.
  3. Click Sign in. Where you land depends on your role — the system decides, you do not choose.
Five failed attempts locks the account for 15 minutes. If you are locked out, wait or ask the Registrar.

The yellow bar at the top

Every page carries a notice reading “Demonstration prototype — not an official TESDA system.” It is deliberate and must not be removed. Without it, browsers classify the site as a deceptive page, because it shows a government agency's name and seal while asking for a password.

Part One

For the applicant

Applicant No account needed · reaches the form at #/apply

Submitting an application

  1. Open the application form. It runs in six steps: Main Details, Additional Details, Contact Details, Diploma Details, Identification Details, and Review.
  2. Fill each step. Fields marked with an asterisk are required; the form will not let you continue past a step that is incomplete.
  3. At Contact Details, give the emergency contact's Last Name, First Name and Middle Name in their separate boxes.
  4. At Identification Details, rename your two files first. The page shows you the exact names to use, built from your own name — for example CRUZ_MARIA_ID and CRUZ_MARIA_BIRTH. Then upload your ID photograph and your birth certificate.
  5. Check everything on the Review step, then submit.
  6. Write down the reference code you are given. It is how you check your status later.
You get one attempt. The two files are filed to the centre's records the moment you submit, and the form cannot be edited afterwards. If the system says you are already on file, you have applied before — go and see the Registrar rather than submitting again.

Part Two

For the Registrar

Registrar Dashboard · Students · Enrollment · Grading Sheets · Academic Catalog · School Years & Semesters · Class Schedules · Grade Evaluation · GSA · Audit Log

1 · Reviewing and approving an application

  1. Open Students and stay on the Pending tab.
  2. If you have just told an applicant to submit, click Refresh to pull in the latest without leaving the page.
  3. Click View on the applicant. The Details tab holds their full record; the Documents tab shows the requirement checklist, which adapts to what they declared themselves to be.
  4. Correct anything wrong by editing inside the same window.
  5. Click Approve, then choose the curriculum and section. Approval is what binds the trainee to a Diploma — from this point the system will not let them be enrolled under any other.
  6. To turn an application down, click Reject and give a reason. The applicant may apply again later.

2 · Enrolling a trainee

  1. Open Enrollment and click Find a student.
  2. Once chosen, the Diploma selector locks to the one they were approved for, and the semester jumps to their own open term. This is intended — it is what prevents enrolling somebody under the wrong Diploma.
  3. Tick the subjects to load, or click Select all available. A subject that cannot be taken is greyed out with the reason beside it.
  4. Click Enroll. The confirmation states the subject count and total units.
  5. To correct a mistake, find the subject under Already enrolled, click Drop and give a reason. A subject that already carries a grade cannot be dropped this way.
If a subject is refused, the message names the reason exactly — for example “Cannot enroll: AUTO-102 was not passed (5.00).” Read it before assuming a fault: the system is enforcing a prerequisite, and every unsatisfied prerequisite is listed, not only the first.

3 · Reviewing grading sheets

  1. Open Grading Sheets. Sheets are grouped by Diploma; each group header shows how many are waiting and how many are incomplete. Click a header to fold it, or Collapse all to fold the lot.
  2. Use the tabs — Submitted, Pending, Approved, All — to narrow the list, and click any column heading to sort.
  3. Click Review on a sheet to see its roster and grades.
  4. Click Approve to accept it. This is the only action that puts grades on a trainee's record.
  5. Click Send back to trainer and state what needs fixing. The trainer reopens it pre-filled and edits rather than re-keying.

4 · Viewing one trainee's whole grading record

  1. From any tab on Grading Sheets, click View a student's grading sheet.
  2. Choose the trainee. Their grades appear grouped by school year and semester, with subject code, subject, prerequisite, units, grade and status.

This is an additional view. It does not replace the per-subject workflow above, and changes nothing.

5 · The Grade Evaluation Form, and resolving an INC

  1. Open Grade Evaluation and click Find a trainee. The form appears immediately — there is nothing to generate.
  2. Click Print form to print. Only the form prints; the sidebar, page heading and panels are left off the paper.
  3. If the trainee has an unresolved INC, an amber panel names the subjects. Click Resolve INC on one.
  4. Choose Completion if the trainee finished the work — the INC stays on the record with the completion grade beside it. Choose Correction only if the INC was recorded in error. Both require remarks.
Why the GWA reads 0.000. An unresolved INC forces it there on purpose. It is telling you the average cannot be relied on yet, not that a calculation failed. Resolve the INC and the figure returns.

6 · Other Registrar tasks

  • Academic Catalog — add and edit Diplomas, curricula, subjects and sections; import a curriculum from CSV.
  • School Years & Semesters — create semesters per Diploma and open or close them. Opening one closes the others for that Diploma and year level.
  • Class Schedules — build the weekly grid, assign trainers and rooms, and publish. Only published classes can be enrolled into. Conflicts are refused.
  • GSA — print a trainee's General Schedule and Assessment for their open semester.
  • Audit Log — every recorded action, searchable and filterable by action and record type.
  • Students → Import CSV — bulk intake. Every bad row is reported in one pass, so you fix them together.

7 · Restoring the demonstration data

  1. On the Dashboard, under Quick actions, click Reset demonstration data.
  2. Type RESET DEMO and your password, then confirm.

This puts the system back to its prepared state: a clean Pending tab, the continuing trainee with their finished semester intact, and every Diploma, curriculum, class and account exactly as before. You stay signed in.

Part Three

For the Trainer

Trainer Grading Sheets · My Schedule — scoped to your own classes only

1 · Your timetable

Open My Schedule for your week — your published classes only, with days, times and rooms.

2 · Encoding and submitting grades

  1. Open Grading Sheets. Every class you teach is listed. Click any column heading to sort — sorting by Status brings the sheets that still need you to the top.
  2. Click Open on a class. View means it is already approved; Fix and resubmit means the Registrar sent it back.
  3. Enter each trainee's grade on the 1.00–5.00 scale — for example 1.50. For anyone without a number, enter INC, DRP or NG.
  4. Click Submit sheet. Note the reference number (GS-…); it is how you and the Registrar refer to the sheet.
Do not enter percentages. The scale is 1.00 to 5.00, where 1.00 is highest and 3.00 is the passing mark — the 75% equivalent. A percentage such as 88 is refused.

3 · A sheet the Registrar sent back

  1. It reappears in your list marked Pending, with the Registrar's remark at the top. Alternatively, type the reference number into Reopen a sheet by reference number and click Open sheet.
  2. The sheet reopens with everything you already entered. Change only what is wrong, then submit again.
If a new name appears on an approved sheet, a trainee joined the class after your submission. The sheet reopens by itself with them added ungraded and a remark naming them. Enter their grade and resubmit — everyone else's grades are untouched.

Part Four

For the Trainee

Trainee Home · My Schedule · My Grades — read only
  • My Schedule — your weekly classes for the open semester.
  • My Grades — the same evaluation the Registrar sees, with your grades, units and weighted average.
  • Home — your student identification and current standing.

You cannot change any of it. If something is wrong, tell the Registrar — corrections are made on their side and recorded in the audit log.

Part Six

When something looks wrong

SymptomCauseFix
A listed account will not sign in The browser is holding an older version of the system Hard-refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R.
You changed something on another computer and cannot see it here Records are held in each browser. Two machines never share data Do the whole demonstration on one computer. This is a known limitation of the prototype.
The review queue is empty No trainer has submitted anything yet Have a trainer submit a sheet — the queue fills at that moment.
The application form hangs on submit The document upload cannot reach the network Abandon it and create the record from Students → Add student.
The data is in a mess after practising Test records left behind Dashboard → Reset demonstration data.
The browser warns the site is dangerous An automated classification of the public address The disclosures are in place; the review is with the provider. Run the system locally if you must avoid it.
What this version does not do. There is no Transcript of Records generator, no completion or graduation list, no document-request tracking, and no reports module. The Grade Evaluation Form is the cumulative academic record for now.
